# Adam’s Big Test

1. When the loud alarm rang, Adam slowly opened his sleepy eyes and whispered, "I have a hard test today, but I am quite ready!"

2. He got out of his warm bed, brushed his teeth carefully, and put on his clean blue school uniform.

3. At breakfast, his happy family ate together and talked about their culture and how working hard always brings great success.

4. Before leaving the house, Adam paused at the front door and thought, "I feel like I can score top marks if I stay very calm and focused."

5. He hugged his parents tightly, said goodbye, and walked outside to wait for the big yellow school bus with his friends.

6. On the morning bus, his good friend played a calm, happy tune on a small guitar to help Adam relax.

7. The long, difficult test questions didn't seem so scary after he took a deep breath and thought very carefully.

8. During the quiet test, Adam focused hard, answered the easy questions first, and then slowly moved to the hard ones.

9. He remembered that at his young age, he can develop great skills so his answers sound clear and good.

10. After acing the school test, Adam happily celebrated with his family by eating a big, delicious hot pizza.
